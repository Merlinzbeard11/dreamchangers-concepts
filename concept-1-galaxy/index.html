Chapter 01 · The Galaxy

A place where imagination isn't a hobby — it's the curriculum.

Inside the Newbicon Galaxy, kids don't just watch a story unfold. They become part of it. Each planet teaches a real-life skill — confidence on Auracle, creativity on Infinatude, kindness in the Forest of Dreams. The lesson hides inside the laughter.

It's the cartoon-network kids actually want, designed by the kind of parents who chose Bluey over CoComelon on purpose.

Chapter 02 · The Heroes

Three pillars. One universe.

Every great galaxy needs three things: a story to follow, a place to learn, and a tool to build. Newbicon is all three.

⌬

The Tooniverse

The animated front door. Weekly episodes built around real-life skills — confidence, creativity, kindness — wrapped in adventure, music, and humor that kids actually want to rewatch.

✺

FutureForge

The kids' Dream Path app. Choose your future-self — Storyteller, Inventor, Tech Builder, Leader — and the app guides you to actually build something real. Lemonade stand. YouTube channel. Charity. Game.

✦

Founders Circle

The community that funds the mission. Patrons help build the network from day one and earn their place on the Wall of Heroes. Belong to something that lasts longer than a season of TV.
