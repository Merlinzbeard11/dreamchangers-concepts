Chapter I

Of attitudes, and the children who shifted them.

In a small town in the foothills of Utah, a writer watched her daughter come home from school with the light gone out of her eyes. She did the only thing a writer can do — she wrote a different ending.

From that page came a galaxy: a place where children learn the ten attitudes that quietly run a life — confidence, kindness, curiosity, courage. Not as homework. As adventure.

Eight books. One mission. A lifetime of stories yet to be told.

A child raised on adventure does not need to be told to be brave. — from the Library of Newbicon
Chapter II

Of MESH — Mental, Emotional, Social Health.

For thirty years, schools called it Social-Emotional Learning. We call it MESH: Mental, Emotional, Social Health. Three circles that overlap inside every child — and inside every story we tell.

Each Newbicon episode hides a MESH lesson inside a hero's choice. Each FutureForge mission turns the lesson into something a child can build. Knowledge becomes practice. Practice becomes character.

This is what we mean by an education that reaches further than a test.
