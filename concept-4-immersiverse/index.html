The MESH Framework

Three circles. One whole human. Every lesson sits where they overlap.

M
Mental

Curiosity. Critical thinking. The muscle that asks why on purpose.

E
Emotional

Self-awareness. Empathy. The ability to feel what is happening and name it.

S
Social

Collaboration. Kindness. Knowing how to be in a room with people who aren't you.
